## Labor and activity

1. **July U.S. employment (BLS; released August 7; observation month July).** Total nonfarm payroll employment changed by -23,000 and the unemployment rate was 4.1%. Local-government education employment fell 50,000, retail trade fell 19,000, and health-care employment continued to trend up. Average hourly earnings for private nonfarm employees were $37.62, up $0.02 in July and 3.2% over 12 months; the average workweek was 34.3 hours. May payroll growth was revised from +129,000 to +63,000 and June from +57,000 to +20,000, a combined downward revision of 103,000. BLS describes the headline changes as little changed; payroll estimates remain subject to sampling error and later revision. Source: https://www.bls.gov/news.release/empsit.nr0.htm

2. **June job openings and turnover (BLS; released August 4; observation month June).** Job openings were 7.4 million with a 4.4% rate; hires were 5.3 million; total separations were 5.4 million; quits were 3.2 million with a 2.0% rate; layoffs and discharges were 1.8 million. BLS described these measures as little changed or unchanged. May openings were revised down by 57,000 to 7.5 million. Source: https://www.bls.gov/news.release/jolts.nr0.htm

3. **Second-quarter productivity (BLS; preliminary release August 6; observation quarter Q2).** Nonfarm-business labor productivity rose at a 1.4% seasonally adjusted annual rate as output rose 1.7% and hours rose 0.3%. Unit labor costs rose 1.3% annualized and 1.4% over four quarters. Real hourly compensation fell 3.1% annualized in Q2. BLS labels these data preliminary; the revised release is scheduled for September 3. Source: https://www.bls.gov/news.release/prod2.nr0.htm

4. **Second-quarter GDP (BEA; advance release July 30; observation quarter Q2).** Real GDP rose at a 1.5% seasonally adjusted annual rate after 2.1% in Q1. Real final sales to private domestic purchasers rose 3.9% annualized. The gross-domestic-purchases price index rose 5.7% annualized, the PCE price index 5.1%, and core PCE prices 3.4%. BEA labels this an advance estimate; the second estimate and corporate-profits release is scheduled for August 26. Source: https://www.bea.gov/news/2026/gdp-advance-estimate-2nd-quarter-2026

5. **July services survey (ISM; released August 5; observation month July).** The Services PMI was 54.1, versus 54.0 in June; business activity was 59.1, new orders 57.2, employment 47.4, and supplier deliveries 52.8. ISM reported a 25th consecutive month in expansion territory for the headline survey index. These are diffusion indexes from a business survey, not comprehensive output or employment counts. Source: https://www.ismworld.org/supply-management-news-and-reports/reports/ism-pmi-reports/services/july/

6. **June retail sales (Census Bureau; released July 16; observation month June).** Advance U.S. retail and food-services sales were $768.6 billion, up 0.2% month over month and 6.7% year over year. Census reported margins of error of ±0.4 percentage point for the monthly change and ±0.5 percentage point for the annual change; the monthly estimate was not statistically distinguishable from zero at the stated confidence level. May growth was revised from 0.9% to 1.0%. Source: https://www.census.gov/retail/sales.html

## Inflation, income, and policy

7. **June CPI (BLS; released July 14; observation month June).** Headline CPI fell 0.4% seasonally adjusted in June and rose 3.5% over 12 months. Core CPI was unchanged in June and rose 2.6% over 12 months. The energy index fell 5.7% in June but was 15.7% above a year earlier; gasoline fell 9.7% in June and was 26.7% above a year earlier. Source: https://www.bls.gov/news.release/cpi.nr0.htm

8. **June PPI (BLS; released July 15; observation month June).** Final-demand PPI fell 0.3% seasonally adjusted in June and rose 5.5% over 12 months. Final-demand goods fell 1.4%, services rose 0.2%, and final demand excluding food, energy, and trade services rose 0.1% in June and 5.1% over 12 months. Source: https://www.bls.gov/news.release/ppi.nr0.htm

9. **June personal income and outlays (BEA; released July 30; observation month June).** Personal income rose 0.2%, disposable personal income rose 0.2%, nominal PCE rose 0.3%, and real PCE rose 0.4%. The PCE price index fell 0.1% in June and rose 3.7% over 12 months; core PCE prices rose 0.1% in June and 3.3% over 12 months. The personal saving rate was 2.7%. BEA says the July release is scheduled for August 26. Source: https://www.bea.gov/news/2026/personal-income-and-outlays-june-2026

10. **June international prices (BLS; released July 17; observation month June).** U.S. import prices rose 0.3% in June and 7.1% over 12 months; export prices fell 0.6% in June. Fuel-import prices fell 0.4% in June but were 44.1% above June 2025. Source: https://www.bls.gov/news.release/ximpim.nr0.htm

11. **Federal Reserve policy (FOMC; statement released July 29).** The FOMC voted 9-3 to maintain the federal-funds target range at 3.50%-3.75%. The statement described economic activity as expanding at a solid pace, inflation as elevated relative to 2%, and uncertainty as elevated in part because of conflict in the Middle East. Three voters preferred a 25-basis-point increase. Source: https://www.federalreserve.gov/newsevents/pressreleases/monetary20260729a.htm

## August 25 releases

### U.S. consumer confidence

- The Conference Board Consumer Confidence Index was 89.4 in August, down 0.8 point from 90.2 in July.
- The Present Situation Index rose 6.8 points to 121.2, while the Expectations Index fell 5.8 points to 68.2.
- The preliminary survey cutoff was August 16. Average and median 12-month inflation expectations rose slightly; 61.3% of respondents expected higher interest rates over the next 12 months, compared with 62.0% in July.

Source: [The Conference Board, August 2026 Consumer Confidence](https://www.conference-board.org/topics/consumer-confidence/index.cfm?os=vbkn42___)

### July new-home sales

- New single-family home sales ran at a seasonally adjusted annual rate of 607,000 in July, down 10.5% from June and 6.3% from July 2025. The Census Bureau states that both changes were within the reported margins of error.
- The seasonally adjusted number of new houses for sale was 488,000, up 1.9% from June and down 1.6% from July 2025.
- Supply was 9.6 months at the current sales rate, compared with 8.5 months in June.
- The median sales price was $393,800, down 2.3% from June and 0.9% from July 2025; both price changes were within the reported margins of error.

Source: [U.S. Census Bureau, July 2026 New Residential Sales](https://www.census.gov/construction/nrs/current/)

## Most recent U.S. macro releases available by the cutoff

- July nonfarm payrolls fell by 23,000 and the unemployment rate was 4.1%. May and June payroll changes were revised down by a combined 103,000.
- July CPI rose 0.1% month over month and 3.4% year over year. Core CPI rose 0.2% month over month and 2.5% year over year. Energy fell 1.5% in July, while food and shelter each rose 0.1%.
- July producer prices for final demand were unchanged month over month and up 4.7% year over year. Services rose 0.2%, construction rose 2.2%, and goods fell 0.7%.
- July retail and food-services sales were $763.6 billion, down 0.6% from June and up 5.0% from July 2025. The estimates are not adjusted for price changes.
- The advance estimate put second-quarter real GDP growth at a 1.5% annual rate, after 2.1% in the first quarter. Real final sales to private domestic purchasers rose 3.9%, compared with 1.7% in the first quarter.
- In June, personal income rose 0.2%, disposable personal income rose 0.2%, current-dollar personal consumption expenditures rose 0.3%, and real personal consumption expenditures rose 0.4%. The personal saving rate was 2.7%. The PCE price index fell 0.1% for the month and was up 3.7% year over year; excluding food and energy, it rose 0.1% for the month and 3.3% year over year.

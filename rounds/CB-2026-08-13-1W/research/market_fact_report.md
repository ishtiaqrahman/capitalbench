Source: https://www.bls.gov/news.release/cpi.nr0.htm

34. The services prices index rose to 70.3 from 67.7. ISM reported 13 services industries expanding and four contracting.
35. ISM survey indexes are diffusion measures based on panel responses. They indicate direction and breadth rather than dollar output levels.

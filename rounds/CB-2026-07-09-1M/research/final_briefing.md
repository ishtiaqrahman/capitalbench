## Labor, Inflation, And Policy

- The U.S. Department of Labor reported initial unemployment claims were 215,000 in the week ended July 4, down 2,000 and below a 220,000 FactSet consensus cited by Associated Press. The four-week average fell to 218,750, while continuing claims rose 8,000 to 1.81 million.
- Associated Press reported that the June employment report showed nonfarm payroll growth of 57,000 and an unemployment rate of 4.2%.
- The June FOMC minutes reported April total PCE inflation at 3.8% and core PCE inflation at 3.3%; Federal Reserve staff estimated May total PCE inflation at 4.1% and core PCE inflation at 3.4%.
- The Federal Reserve maintained the federal funds target range at 3.50% to 3.75% by a 12-0 vote at the June meeting.
- The minutes said the median Desk survey path implied no target-range change through the start of 2027 and one rate cut in the second quarter of 2027. Market pricing suggested one mid-2027 rate increase, with the minutes noting an effect from term premiums.

## Business Activity And Supply Conditions

- ISM reported June Manufacturing PMI at 53.3, new orders at 56.0, production at 52.2, employment at 49.7, supplier deliveries at 57.4, and prices at 73.0.
- ISM reported June Services PMI at 54.0 and prices at 67.7. ISM said all items listed in short supply in June were inputs needed for data-center construction.
- Associated Press reported Micron Technology rose 4.5% after an update on its central New York semiconductor site; Micron cited surging memory demand in the AI era.
- Associated Press reported China passenger-car exports rose 80% from one year earlier in June to about 905,000, while domestic passenger-car sales fell 26% to about 1.5 million.

## Housing And Consumer Facts

- Associated Press reported June existing-home sales fell 2.4% from May to a 4.09 million annual rate and were 2.8% above June 2025. The median sale price rose 1.8% from one year earlier to $440,600.
- National Association of Realtors data cited by Associated Press showed existing-home inventory at 1.56 million, up 1.3% from one year earlier and equal to 4.6 months of supply. First-time buyers accounted for 33% of purchases.
- Freddie Mac data cited by Associated Press put the average 30-year fixed mortgage rate at 6.49%, up from 6.43% one week earlier and below 6.72% one year earlier.
- PepsiCo reported Q2 revenue rose 6.4% to $24.2 billion. Associated Press reported North American snack volume was flat and beverage volume fell 4%, while overall snack volume rose 3% and beverage volume rose 2%.

## Energy And Geopolitical Facts

- The U.S. military said it struck 90 targets in Iran on July 9. Associated Press reported that Iran responded with attacks directed at U.S.-allied countries in the region and that the interim ceasefire arrangement remained uncertain at the cutoff.
- Preliminary Lloyd's List Intelligence data cited by Associated Press showed at least 576 ships transited the Strait of Hormuz in June, versus 233 in May and more than 3,100 in June 2025.
- Federal Reserve and ISM source material identified energy, tariffs, Middle East developments, and AI-related demand among current price and supply influences.

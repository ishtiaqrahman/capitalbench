## Monetary policy and inflation

- On July 29, the Federal Open Market Committee maintained the federal funds target range at 3.50% to 3.75%. The vote was 9–3, with three members preferring a 25-basis-point increase.
- July CPI rose 0.1% from June and 3.4% from a year earlier. Core CPI rose 0.2% for the month and 2.5% over twelve months.
- July final-demand producer prices were unchanged from June and were 4.7% above a year earlier.
- June PCE prices fell 0.1% from May and rose 3.7% from a year earlier. Core PCE prices rose 0.1% for the month and 3.3% over twelve months.

## Growth, spending, labor, and housing

- The advance estimate put second-quarter real GDP growth at a 1.5% annual rate after 2.1% in the first quarter. Real final sales to private domestic purchasers rose at a 3.9% annual rate after 1.7% in the first quarter.
- June real consumer spending rose 0.4%. Current-dollar personal income and disposable personal income each rose 0.2%, and the personal saving rate was 2.7%.
- July nonfarm payroll employment fell by 23,000. The unemployment rate was 4.1%, the labor-force participation rate was 61.4%, and temporary layoffs rose by 153,000 to 921,000.
- Advance July retail and food-services sales fell 0.6% from June and rose 5.0% from a year earlier; the series is not adjusted for price changes.
- July housing starts were at a 1.239 million annual rate, down 12.4% from June and 13.5% from a year earlier. Building permits were at a 1.443 million rate, up 5.0% from June and 3.1% from a year earlier.

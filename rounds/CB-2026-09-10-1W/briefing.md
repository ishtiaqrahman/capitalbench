## U.S. activity and spending

- BEA, August 26 release, second estimate: second-quarter real GDP grew at a 1.5% annualized rate, following 2.1% in the first quarter. Consumer spending, exports and investment increased; government spending decreased and imports increased.
- BEA, August 26 release, July estimates: personal income rose 0.4% month over month, nominal consumption rose 0.2%, and real consumption increased less than 0.1%. The personal saving rate was 3.0%.

## Employment and compensation

- BLS, September 4 release, August preliminary estimates: payroll employment increased 162,000 and unemployment remained 4.1%. Average hourly earnings rose 0.3% month over month and 3.1% year over year. June and July payroll changes were revised upward by a combined 55,000.
- BLS, September 9 release, June compensation-cost estimates: civilian employers paid $49.46 per employee hour, including $33.85 in wages and $15.61 in benefits. Private-industry compensation was $46.89 per hour. These are compensation levels, not growth rates.

## U.S. consumer prices

- BEA, August 26 release, July PCE prices: headline and food-and-energy-excluded indexes each increased 0.2% month over month; annual increases were 3.7% and 3.3%, respectively. August CPI and PCE results were not available at the cutoff.

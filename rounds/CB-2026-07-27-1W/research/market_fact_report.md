- BLS reported on July 2 that June nonfarm payroll employment increased by 57,000 and the unemployment rate was 4.2%. Labor-force participation fell 0.3 percentage point to 61.5%. Average hourly earnings rose 0.3% on the month and 3.5% over twelve months. BLS scheduled the July employment report for August 7 at 8:30 a.m. Eastern.
- The Census Bureau reported on July 16 that June retail and food-services sales were $768.6 billion, up 0.2% from May and 6.7% from June 2025. The monthly estimate carried a ±0.4 percentage-point sampling margin, so Census did not establish that the monthly change differed from zero.
- The Census Bureau reported on July 27 that June durable-goods orders rose 0.3% to $334.8 billion after a revised 4.0% May decline. Orders excluding transportation rose 0.6%; orders excluding defense rose 0.3%; computers and electronic products rose 3.1% to $31.1 billion.
- Census and HUD reported on July 24 that June new-home sales ran at a seasonally adjusted annual rate of 628,000, 1.6% above May and 5.6% below June 2025. The stated sampling margins were ±14.8% and ±13.2%, respectively, so neither comparison was statistically conclusive. Inventory represented 9.3 months of supply. The median sale price was $398,300, with Census also reporting wide sampling uncertainty around price changes.

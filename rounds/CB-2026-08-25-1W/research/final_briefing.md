## August 25 market close

- The S&P 500 rose 0.3% to 7,677.28, the Dow Jones Industrial Average rose 0.3% to 53,577.40, the Nasdaq composite rose 0.7% to 26,151.30, and the Russell 2000 rose 0.5% to 3,010.02.
- Brent crude fell 3.6% to $87.27 per barrel.
- The 10-year U.S. Treasury yield was 4.63%, compared with 4.70% on August 24 and 4.74% on August 21.
- Nvidia gained 2.2%. Dick's Sporting Goods fell 30.7% after reporting weaker results and lowering its forecast.
- South Korea's Kospi rose 0.7% on August 25; it had fallen 22.2% during July.

## August 25 releases

- The Conference Board Consumer Confidence Index was 89.4 in August, down from 90.2 in July. Its Present Situation Index rose to 121.2, while its Expectations Index fell to 68.2. The preliminary survey cutoff was August 16.
- July new single-family home sales were at a seasonally adjusted annual rate of 607,000, down 10.5% from June and 6.3% from July 2025; both changes were within the reported margins of error.
- New houses for sale totaled a seasonally adjusted 488,000. Supply was 9.6 months at the current sales rate, compared with 8.5 months in June. The median sales price was $393,800.

## Latest available U.S. macro data

- July nonfarm payrolls fell by 23,000, the unemployment rate was 4.1%, and May and June payroll changes were revised down by a combined 103,000.
- July CPI rose 0.1% month over month and 3.4% year over year. Core CPI rose 0.2% month over month and 2.5% year over year.
- July producer prices for final demand were unchanged month over month and up 4.7% year over year.
- July retail and food-services sales fell 0.6% from June and rose 5.0% from July 2025. The estimates are not adjusted for price changes.
- The advance estimate put second-quarter real GDP growth at a 1.5% annual rate, following 2.1% in the first quarter. Real final sales to private domestic purchasers rose 3.9%.
- In June, personal income and disposable personal income each rose 0.2%, current-dollar consumer spending rose 0.3%, and real consumer spending rose 0.4%. The personal saving rate was 2.7%. The PCE price index fell 0.1% for the month and rose 3.7% year over year; excluding food and energy, it rose 0.1% for the month and 3.3% year over year.

## Policy and energy

- On July 29, the Federal Open Market Committee maintained the federal-funds target range at 3.50% to 3.75%. The vote was 9-3; three members preferred a 25-basis-point increase.
- U.S. crude-oil stocks including the Strategic Petroleum Reserve were 722.241 million barrels for the week ended August 14, compared with 723.104 million barrels one week earlier.

## 3. U.S. growth, income, spending, and prices

BEA's August 26 second estimate put second-quarter real GDP growth at a 1.5% seasonally adjusted annual rate, unchanged at the displayed precision from the advance estimate, after 2.1% in the first quarter. Real final sales to private domestic purchasers rose at a 4.2% annual rate, revised up from 3.9%. Real GDI rose at a 2.2% rate. The gross domestic purchases price index rose at a 5.8% rate, the PCE price index at 5.3%, and core PCE at 3.6%. Corporate profits from current production increased by $400.9 billion after a $74.4 billion increase in the first quarter.

BEA's July personal-income release showed personal income up 0.4% month over month, disposable personal income up 0.5%, and nominal PCE up 0.2%. Real disposable income rose 0.4%, while real PCE changed by less than 0.1%. The personal saving rate was 3.0%. The PCE price index rose 0.2% in July and 3.7% from a year earlier; core PCE rose 0.2% and 3.3%, respectively. The nominal spending composition was a $86.2 billion increase in services and a $49.9 billion decrease in goods.

The July CPI rose 0.1% month over month and 3.4% year over year. Core CPI rose 0.2% and 2.5%, respectively. Shelter rose 0.1% in July, food rose 0.1%, and energy fell 1.5%. The July PPI for final demand was unchanged month over month and rose 4.7% year over year; final-demand services rose 0.2% and goods fell 0.7% for the month.

## 5. U.S. production, trade, retail, housing, and inventories

July industrial production and manufacturing production each rose 0.2% after 0.3% increases in June. Total industrial production was 1.1% above July 2025, and total capacity utilization edged up to 76.3%, 3.1 percentage points below its 1972–2025 average. Business-equipment output rose 0.8%; consumer-goods output fell 0.4%.

Advance July retail and food-services sales were $763.6 billion, down 0.6% month over month and up 5.0% year over year. The estimates are adjusted for seasonal, holiday, and trading-day effects but not price changes.

Advance July goods data showed a $118.8 billion deficit, widening by $17.4 billion from June. Exports fell $6.0 billion to $199.4 billion, while imports rose $11.4 billion to $318.2 billion. Advance wholesale inventories rose 1.3% month over month and 5.7% year over year; advance retail inventories rose 0.7% and 3.8%, respectively.

July housing starts were at a 1.239 million seasonally adjusted annual rate, down 12.4% from revised June and 13.5% from a year earlier. Building permits were at a 1.443 million rate, up 5.0% from revised June and 3.1% from a year earlier. Single-family starts were 808,000 and single-family permits were 894,000. Census identifies several of the reported monthly changes as statistically uncertain at the published confidence interval.

## Source ledger and extracted facts

### 1. Federal Reserve policy

- **Publisher:** Board of Governors of the Federal Reserve System
- **Page:** Federal Reserve issues FOMC statement
- **Publication and observation date:** July 29, 2026
- **URL:** https://www.federalreserve.gov/newsevents/pressreleases/monetary20260729a.htm
- **Facts:** The FOMC maintained the federal-funds target range at 3.50%-3.75% and continued its policy of maintaining ample reserves. The vote was 9-3; Beth Hammack, Neel Kashkari, and Lorie Logan preferred a 25-basis-point increase. The statement described economic activity as expanding at a solid pace, job gains as keeping pace with the workforce, and inflation as elevated relative to the 2% goal. It identified elevated uncertainty partly related to the Middle East conflict and supply-shock price increases including energy.
- **Source-reported uncertainty/status:** The statement expressly characterized uncertainty as elevated. The three dissents document disagreement over the appropriate policy setting.

### 3. Second-quarter GDP and prices

- **Publisher:** U.S. Bureau of Economic Analysis
- **Page:** GDP (Advance Estimate), 2nd Quarter 2026
- **Publication date:** July 30, 2026
- **Observation period:** April-June 2026
- **URL:** https://www.bea.gov/news/2026/gdp-advance-estimate-2nd-quarter-2026
- **Facts:** Real GDP increased at a 1.5% seasonally adjusted annual rate after 2.1% in the first quarter. Real final sales to private domestic purchasers increased 3.9%. Consumer spending, investment, and exports contributed positively; government spending decreased and imports increased. The gross-domestic-purchases price index increased at a 5.7% annual rate, the PCE price index at 5.1%, and the PCE price index excluding food and energy at 3.4%.
- **Source-reported uncertainty/status:** This was the advance estimate. BEA scheduled the second estimate and corporate-profits release for August 26 at 8:30 a.m. EDT.

- **Publisher:** U.S. Bureau of Economic Analysis
- **Page:** Personal Income and Outlays, June 2026
- **Publication date:** July 30, 2026
- **Observation period:** June 2026
- **URL:** https://www.bea.gov/news/2026/personal-income-and-outlays-june-2026

### 5. Productivity and labor costs

- **Publisher:** U.S. Bureau of Labor Statistics
- **Page:** Productivity and Costs, Second Quarter 2026, Preliminary
- **Publication date:** August 6, 2026
- **Observation period:** Second quarter 2026
- **URL:** https://www.bls.gov/news.release/prod2.nr0.htm
- **Facts:** Nonfarm-business productivity increased at a 1.4% annual rate as output rose 1.7% and hours rose 0.3%. Unit labor costs increased 1.3%. From a year earlier, productivity rose 2.2% and unit labor costs rose 1.4%. Real hourly compensation decreased at a 3.1% annual rate in the quarter. BLS reported a 52.9% labor share, the lowest reading in the series beginning in 1947. Manufacturing productivity increased 1.9%, with durable-manufacturing productivity up 2.7% and nondurable-manufacturing productivity up 2.0%.
- **Source-reported uncertainty/status:** The data were preliminary. Revised second-quarter figures were scheduled for September 3 at 8:30 a.m. EDT; first-quarter productivity was revised to 0.8% from 0.3%.

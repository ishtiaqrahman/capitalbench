## U.S. policy and economic releases

- On July 29, the Federal Open Market Committee kept the federal-funds target at 3.50%-3.75% by a 9-3 vote. Three voters preferred a 25-basis-point increase. The statement said inflation remained elevated relative to 2%.

- June payroll employment rose by 57,000 and unemployment was 4.2%; BLS described both as little changed.

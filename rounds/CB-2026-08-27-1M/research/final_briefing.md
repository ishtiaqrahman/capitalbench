## Rates and Monetary Policy

- U.S. Treasury August 27 nominal par-yield estimates were 3.81% at one month, 3.94% at six months, 4.20% at two years, 4.38% at five years, 4.67% at ten years, and 5.19% at thirty years. The two-, ten-, and thirty-year estimates were each one basis point lower on August 26. Treasury identifies the series as daily estimates derived from indicative market quotations.
- The Federal Reserve maintained the federal-funds target range at 3.50%–3.75% on July 29 by a 9–3 vote; three members preferred a 25-basis-point increase. The statement described economic activity as expanding at a solid pace and inflation as elevated.

## Prices, Income, and Growth

- The Bureau of Economic Analysis reported July personal income up 0.4%, disposable income up 0.5%, nominal consumption up 0.2%, real disposable income up 0.4%, real consumption effectively unchanged, and a 3.0% saving rate. PCE prices rose 0.2% month over month and 3.7% year over year; excluding food and energy they rose 0.2% and 3.3%.
- BEA's August 26 second estimate put second-quarter real GDP growth at a 1.5% annual rate, real final sales to private domestic purchasers at 4.2%, real gross domestic income at 2.2%, and current-production corporate-profit growth at $400.9 billion. The estimates are subject to revision.
- The Bureau of Labor Statistics reported July CPI up 0.1% month over month and 3.4% year over year; CPI excluding food and energy rose 0.2% and 2.5%. July final-demand PPI was unchanged month over month and up 4.7% year over year; final demand excluding food, energy, and trade services rose 0.4% and 4.7%.

## Labor, Production, and Demand

- BLS reported July payroll employment down 23,000, unemployment at 4.1%, and 6.9 million people unemployed. May and June payroll levels were revised down by a combined 103,000. Survey estimates are subject to sampling and nonsampling error and revision.
- BLS reported June job openings at 7.4 million, hires at 5.3 million, separations at 5.4 million, quits at 3.2 million, and layoffs and discharges at 1.8 million; it described all five as little changed.
- The Federal Reserve reported July industrial production up 0.2% month over month and 1.1% year over year, manufacturing up 0.2%, business-equipment output up 0.8%, consumer-goods output down 0.4%, and capacity utilization at 76.3%. The release is preliminary and scheduled for revision.
- The Census Bureau reported advance July retail and food-services sales down 0.6% month over month with a ±0.4 percentage-point sampling error and up 5.0% year over year with a ±0.5-point error. Advance durable-goods orders rose 1.1%, including 0.4% excluding transportation and 1.3% excluding defense.
- The Census Bureau reported an advance July goods deficit of $118.8 billion, $17.4 billion wider than June, with exports down $6.0 billion and imports up $11.4 billion. Advance wholesale inventories rose 1.3% month over month with a ±0.2-point sampling error, and advance retail inventories rose 0.7% with a ±0.2-point error; advance estimates are revised with fuller data.

## Housing and Energy

- The Census Bureau and Department of Housing and Urban Development reported July permits at a 1.443 million annual rate, up 5.0% month over month, and starts at a 1.239 million rate, down 12.4% with a ±9.5 percentage-point interval. Single-family starts fell 9.9% with a ±10.4-point interval, which was not statistically significant at that interval.
- July new-home sales were at a 607,000 annual rate, down 10.5% with a ±14.0 percentage-point interval; supply was 488,000 homes or 9.6 months. The monthly and annual sales changes were not statistically significant at the reported intervals.
- The Energy Information Administration reported total crude stocks including the Strategic Petroleum Reserve down 3.605 million barrels in the week ended August 21, commercial crude stocks up 0.095 million, the reserve down 3.700 million, production at 13.843 million barrels per day, and refinery utilization at 97.4%. Weekly values are estimates and can be revised.
- EIA reported working natural gas in storage at 3,184 billion cubic feet for the week ended August 21, up 15 billion from the prior week. Weekly values are estimates and can be revised.

## International Data

- The European Central Bank left its key rates unchanged on July 23 at 2.25% for deposits, 2.40% for main refinancing operations, and 2.65% for marginal lending. It stated that decisions would remain data-dependent and meeting-by-meeting.
- Eurostat reported final July euro-area inflation of 2.9% year over year, with services contributing 1.55 percentage points and energy 0.94 point. Its preliminary flash estimate put second-quarter euro-area GDP growth at 0.4% quarter over quarter and employment growth at 0.1%; preliminary estimates can be revised.
- China's National Bureau of Statistics reported a July manufacturing PMI of 49.2, a non-manufacturing business-activity index of 49.0, and a composite PMI output index of 49.3. July industrial value added rose 4.5% year over year, including 5.5% for manufacturing.

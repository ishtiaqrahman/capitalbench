## Rates, Growth, Inflation, and Labor

- The FOMC voted 9-3 on July 29 to maintain the federal-funds target range at 3.50% to 3.75%; three members preferred a 25-basis-point increase. It described activity as solid, unemployment as little changed, inflation as elevated relative to 2%, and outlook uncertainty as elevated.
- The ten-year U.S. Treasury yield was 4.67% late July 30, unchanged from late July 29; the thirty-year yield rose to 5.22% from 5.20%.
- Advance second-quarter real GDP increased at a 1.5% annual rate after 2.1% in the first quarter. Real final sales to private domestic purchasers rose 3.9%. The gross-domestic-purchases price index increased 5.7%, the PCE price index 5.1%, and core PCE 3.4%, all at annual rates.
- In June, personal income rose 0.2%, nominal PCE 0.3%, real PCE 0.4%, and the saving rate was 2.7%. The PCE price index fell 0.1% from May and rose 3.7% over twelve months; core PCE rose 0.1% and 3.3%, respectively.
- June CPI fell 0.4% from May and rose 3.5% over twelve months. Core CPI was unchanged on the month and rose 2.6% over twelve months. June final-demand PPI fell 0.3% and rose 5.5% over twelve months.
- Initial unemployment claims were 197,000 for the week ended July 25, up 9,000. Insured unemployment was 1.782 million and the insured unemployment rate was 1.2%.
- June payrolls increased by 57,000, unemployment was 4.2%, and labor-force participation was 61.5%. April and May payroll estimates were revised down by a combined 74,000.

- Microsoft reported quarterly revenue up 18%, cloud revenue up 27%, and Azure growth of 43%; More Personal Computing revenue fell 4%. Meta reported revenue up 28%, costs and expenses up 55%, operating income down 8%, and net income down 14%.
- Apple reported quarterly revenue of $109.4 billion, up 16%, gross margin of 50.1%, and diluted EPS of $2.02, up 29%. Tariff refunds added approximately 2 percentage points to gross margin and $0.11 to EPS.

Values are seasonally adjusted but not adjusted for prices and can be revised.

## Policy and cross-asset observations

Most participants assessed that another increase would likely be appropriate by year end, while emphasizing that subsequent decisions depend on incoming information. Participants described solid activity and elevated inflation; several also noted pressure on lower-income households from energy costs. The minutes describe the September meeting, not a new October policy decision.

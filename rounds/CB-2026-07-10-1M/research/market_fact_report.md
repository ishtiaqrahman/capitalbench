## Market Close And Cross-Asset Regime

- On Friday, July 10, the S&P 500 rose 31.75 points, or 0.4%, to 7,575.39; the Dow Jones Industrial Average rose 149.60 points, or 0.3%, to 52,367.01; and the Nasdaq Composite rose 74.72 points, or 0.3%, to 26,281.61.
- The S&P 500 completed its fourth winning week in five.
- Brent crude fell 0.4% to $76.01 a barrel. It was above approximately $72 at the start of the week and below its wartime peak near $120.
- The 10-year Treasury yield rose to 4.56% from 4.54% late July 9.
- Nvidia rose 4% and was the largest single contributor lifting the S&P 500 on July 10.
- Internationally, South Korea's Kospi rose 2.5%, Japan's Nikkei 225 rose 1.2%, and Shanghai fell 1.0%.

## Labor, Inflation, And Monetary Policy

- Initial claims for unemployment benefits were 215,000 in the week ended July 4, down 2,000 and below the 220,000 FactSet consensus cited by Associated Press.
- The four-week average of initial claims fell 3,750 to 218,750. Continuing claims for the week ended June 27 rose 8,000 to 1.81 million.
- The June employment report showed nonfarm payroll growth of 57,000 and an unemployment rate of 4.2%. Associated Press reported that the unemployment-rate decline from 4.3% mostly reflected people leaving the labor force.
- The June FOMC minutes reported April total PCE inflation at 3.8% and core PCE inflation at 3.3%. Federal Reserve staff estimated May total PCE inflation at 4.1% and core PCE inflation at 3.4%.
- The Federal Reserve maintained the federal funds target range at 3.50% to 3.75% by a unanimous vote at the June meeting.
- The median Desk survey path implied no target-range change through the start of 2027 and one rate cut in the second quarter of 2027. Market pricing suggested one mid-2027 rate increase, while the minutes noted that term premiums likely affected that measure.
- Federal Reserve staff described forecast uncertainty as elevated, with risks to employment and real GDP tilted somewhat downward and risks to inflation skewed upward.

## Technology, Corporate, And Capital-Market Datapoints

- SK Hynix American depositary receipts were priced at $149, opened at $170, and closed at $168.01, a 12.8% gain. The sale of 177.9 million receipts raised $26.5 billion.
- Associated Press reported that SK Hynix has a dominant position in high-bandwidth memory, that the United States represented 68.8% of its 2025 revenue, and that memory-chip prices had risen as AI-related demand outpaced supply.
- The same report said 48 U.S. initial public offerings raised $104.8 billion in the second quarter, the highest quarterly proceeds in five years, including $75 billion from SpaceX.
- Associated Press reported that Delta Air Lines' spring profit and revenue exceeded analyst expectations and that the midpoint of its summer profit forecast exceeded analyst expectations. Delta shares fell 1.8% after having risen 28.2% year to date before July 10.
- Circle Internet Group rose 5% after announcing U.S. regulatory approval to establish Circle National Trust. WD-40 rose 10.6% after reporting quarterly profit above analyst expectations.
- TSMC's public monthly-revenue page showed January-May 2026 consolidated net revenue of NT$1,961.804 billion, up 30.0% from the comparable prior-year period. The page labeled the 2026 figures unaudited and did not display a June value at the research cutoff.

## Energy And Geopolitical Datapoints

- The July 10 Associated Press live report said senior U.S. officials demanded that Iran publicly state that traffic through the Strait of Hormuz had been fully restored and that Iranian forces would stop attacking ships.
- The report said U.S. officials described progress in talks with Iranian leaders while also attributing new attacks in the strait to an internal Iranian power struggle. The officials spoke on condition of anonymity.
- Iran's U.N. diplomat said Iran remained committed to the ceasefire framework if the United States complied with its obligations. President Donald Trump said talks would continue but stated that the ceasefire was no longer in place.
- Brent crude ended above its level at the start of the week while remaining below the wartime peak reported by Associated Press.

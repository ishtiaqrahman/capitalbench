index closes, breadth, qualitative rates, energy flows, international policy,
and scheduled catalysts are covered. Quantitative current Treasury, FX, metals,
or country-release figures without reviewed evidence are not inserted. Complete
mechanical data supplies neutral cross-asset price coverage. No selected-return
table, Q1 rank/score summary, candidate-slate interpretation, option ranking,
recommendation, or expected-return mapping is included. Refer to complete
market_data/universe_decision_context.md and separately generated quality
evidence. The two horizons share the factual briefing but have different frozen

## Labor and domestic activity

- BLS's August employment release, reported by CNBC on September 4, showed
  seasonally adjusted payroll growth of 162,000 and unchanged unemployment of
  4.1%. June and July payroll gains were revised to 31,000 and 21,000. Average
  hourly earnings rose 0.3% monthly and 3.1% annually. Payroll estimates remain
  subject to revision.
- BEA's August 26 second estimate put second-quarter real GDP growth at 1.5%
  annualized, following 2.1% in the first quarter. Real final sales to private
  domestic purchasers grew 4.2% annualized, revised upward by 0.3 percentage
  point. These are quarterly annualized measures.

## Prices and household spending

- BEA reported July headline and core PCE price increases of 0.2% each from
  June. Their year-over-year increases were 3.7% and 3.3%, respectively.
- BEA reported July nominal consumption growth of 0.2%, real consumption
  growth below 0.1%, disposable personal income growth of 0.5%, and a 3.0%
  personal saving rate. The release revised April–June estimates.

## Monetary policy and rates

- On July 29 the Federal Reserve retained the 3.50%–3.75% federal-funds target
  range by a 9–3 vote. The dissenting voters preferred a 0.25-percentage-point
  increase. The statement described solid activity and inflation above 2%.
- CNBC's September 4 Treasury report, updated at 16:12 EDT, quoted two-year,
  ten-year, and thirty-year yields of 4.377%, 4.784%, and 5.245%. These are
  article-time quotes. It reported CME FedWatch pricing of a 58% probability
  of a quarter-point September increase; that is a market-implied estimate,
  not a policy decision.

## Equity-market observations

- CNBC reported September 4 closes of 53,414.25 for the Dow, 7,718.60 for the
  S&P 500, and 26,506.99 for the Nasdaq Composite. Their session changes were
  −0.51%, −0.38%, and −0.29%, respectively. Exchange-wide advancing/declining
  issue counts and a current credit-spread observation are not supplied.

## International activity and prices

- Eurostat's August flash estimate, reported September 1 by CNBC, put annual
  euro-area inflation at 3.3%, versus 2.9% in July. Energy inflation increased
  to 14.3% from 10.3%, while core inflation decreased to 2.4% from 2.5%.
- China's official August manufacturing PMI was 49.8, versus 49.2 in July,
  according to NBS data reported August 31 in China by CNBC. Production was
  50.4, new orders 50.6, and new export orders 50.1; the headline was below
  50 while those components were above 50.

## Energy and trade

- CNBC's September 4 energy report put U.S. nationwide diesel at $5.85 per
  gallon, versus $3.71 in its year-earlier comparison. It reported refinery
  interruptions in Russia and the Middle East.
- BEA and Census reported a July nominal goods-and-services deficit of $88.6
  billion, versus revised June $71.2 billion. July exports were $310.7 billion
  and imports $399.3 billion. The year-to-date deficit was 29.6% below the
  comparable 2025 period. The figures are seasonally adjusted, not price-adjusted.
- On September 4 President Trump threatened to stop trade with countries
  having trade surpluses with the U.S. unless the Federal Reserve cut rates,
  CNBC reported. The report provided no implementation date or confirmation
  of an enacted blanket trade halt.

## U.S. economy and inflation

- BEA reported that August personal income rose 0.2% month over month, disposable personal income rose 0.3%, nominal PCE rose 0.9%, and real PCE rose 0.6%. The personal saving rate was 4.1%.
- The August PCE price index rose 0.3% month over month and 3.4% year over year. Excluding food and energy, it rose 0.2% month over month and 3.0% year over year.
- BEA's September 30 annual update revised historical national-account estimates beginning with January 2021.
- BEA's third estimate put second-quarter real GDP growth at a 2.2% annualized rate; first-quarter growth was revised to 2.5%. Consumer spending, investment, and exports contributed positively, while imports increased.

## Labor market

- August nonfarm payrolls increased by 162,000 and unemployment was unchanged at 4.1%. Labor-force participation was 61.6%.
- Average hourly earnings rose 0.3% in August and 3.1% over twelve months. BLS revised the combined June and July payroll change upward by 55,000.
- Monthly labor estimates are survey-based and remain subject to revision.

## Monetary policy and rates

- On September 16, the Federal Reserve raised the federal-funds target range by 25 basis points to 3.75%–4.00% in a 12–0 vote.
- September FOMC median projections for 2026 were 2.3% real-GDP growth, 4.1% unemployment, 3.7% PCE inflation, 3.4% core PCE inflation, and a 4.1% year-end federal-funds rate. These are participant projections, not a committed policy path.
- At the September 30 close, U.S. Treasury par yields were 4.88% at two years, 5.29% at ten years, and 5.64% at thirty years.

## Market and cross-asset observations

- On September 30, the S&P 500 fell 0.3% to 7,651.54, the Dow fell 0.9% to 50,906.05, the Nasdaq Composite rose 0.2% to 26,861.06, and the Russell 2000 fell 0.4% to 2,796.86. Most U.S. stocks declined during the session.
- U.S. commercial crude inventories excluding the Strategic Petroleum Reserve were 427.320 million barrels for the week ended September 25, up 0.922 million from the prior week and 10.774 million from the comparable 2025 week.
- EIA's September forecast placed U.S. distillate inventories below 100 million barrels in September and below their 2021–2025 seasonal low through the end of 2026 and most of 2027. This is a conditional forecast.

## International conditions

- China's official September manufacturing PMI was 50.1, with production at 51.7 and new orders at 50.5. Large-enterprise PMI was 50.6; medium and small enterprises were at 49.7 and 48.9.
- The ECB raised its three key rates by 25 basis points in September, setting the deposit facility at 2.50%, the main refinancing rate at 2.65%, and the marginal lending rate at 2.90%.
- ECB staff projected headline inflation of 3.0% in 2026 and 2.5% in 2027, and real growth of 0.9% in 2026 and 1.4% in 2027. The ECB described the outlook as highly uncertain, with upside inflation and downside growth risks.

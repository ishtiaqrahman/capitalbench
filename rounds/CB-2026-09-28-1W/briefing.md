## U.S. listed markets

At the September 25 close, the S&P 500 was 7,743.41, the Dow Jones Industrial Average was 51,828.62, the Nasdaq Composite was 27,068.72, and the Russell 2000 was 2,837.55. For the week ending September 25, the S&P 500 rose 1.2%, the Dow 0.3%, and the Nasdaq 2.1%; the Russell 2000 fell 0.8%. NYSE Research described breadth as weaker over the week: the capitalization-weighted S&P 500 rose about 1%, while the equal-weight S&P 500 and small- and mid-cap indexes moved slightly lower.

## Rates, inflation, and economic activity

On September 25, U.S. Treasury par yields were 4.81% at two years, 5.17% at ten years, and 5.49% at thirty years. On September 16, the Federal Reserve raised the federal funds target range by 0.25 percentage point to 3.75%–4.00% in a 12–0 vote.

In August, CPI rose 0.4% seasonally adjusted from July; its 12-month increase was 3.4%. CPI excluding food and energy rose 0.3% for the month and 2.4% over twelve months. Final-demand PPI rose 0.4% seasonally adjusted in August and 5.4% unadjusted over twelve months; final demand excluding food, energy, and trade services rose 0.3% for the month and 4.7% over twelve months. BLS revised April–July PPI figures for late reports and respondent corrections.

August nonfarm payroll employment increased by 162,000 and unemployment was 4.1%; BLS revised June and July payroll growth upward by a combined 55,000. The second estimate of real GDP growth for 2026 Q2 was 1.5% at an annual rate. July personal income rose 0.4% and personal consumption expenditures rose 0.2%. ISM's August Manufacturing PMI was 54.6; its Services PMI was 55.4, with a Services Employment Index of 47.8 and Prices Index of 72.6. These are diffusion-index readings from ISM survey panels.

## U.S. market close and cross-asset snapshot

- The Associated Press reported that on August 26 the S&P 500 fell 1.58 points to 7,675.70, the Dow Jones Industrial Average fell 113.52 points to 53,463.88, the Nasdaq Composite fell 21.10 points to 26,130.20, and the Russell 2000 fell 4.12 points to 3,005.90. ## Inflation, income, consumption, and output

- The Bureau of Economic Analysis released July personal income and outlays on August 26. Personal income rose 0.4% month over month, disposable personal income rose 0.5%, current-dollar personal consumption expenditures rose 0.2%, and the personal saving rate was 3.0%.
- The July PCE price index rose 0.2% month over month and 3.7% year over year. Core PCE prices excluding food and energy rose 0.2% month over month and 3.3% year over year. AP reported the 3.7% headline rate was unchanged from June and above the 3.6% FactSet economist consensus.
- BEA's second estimate put second-quarter real GDP growth at a 1.5% annual rate, unchanged from the advance estimate and down from 2.1% in the first quarter. Real final sales to private domestic purchasers rose at a 4.2% annual rate. Real gross domestic income rose 2.2%.
- BEA reported second-quarter PCE price inflation at a 5.3% annual rate and core PCE inflation at 3.6%. Profits from current production rose by $400.9 billion after a $74.4 billion first-quarter increase.

## Labor, manufacturing, and housing

- The Bureau of Labor Statistics' July Employment Situation, released August 7, recorded a 23,000 decline in nonfarm payroll employment and a 4.1% unemployment rate. May and June payroll changes were revised down by a combined 103,000. BLS described both July payroll employment and the unemployment rate as little changed.
- The Commerce Department's July durable-goods report, released August 26, recorded a 1.1% monthly increase in new orders to $339.3 billion. Orders excluding transportation rose 0.4%, orders excluding defense rose 1.3%, and transportation-equipment orders rose 2.3%. The Census Bureau states that these estimates are not based on a probability sample and do not have computable sampling errors.
- July new single-family home sales fell 10.5% from June to a seasonally adjusted annual rate of 607,000 and were 6.3% below a year earlier, according to Census and HUD data summarized by the National Association of Home Builders. Inventory was 488,000 units, equal to 9.6 months of supply; the median sales price was $393,800. A new-home sale is recorded at contract signing or deposit and can involve a home at any construction stage.

## Monetary policy and rates

- On July 29 the Federal Open Market Committee maintained the federal-funds target range at 3.50% to 3.75% by a 9–3 vote. The three dissents preferred a 25-basis-point increase. The statement said inflation remained elevated relative to the 2% goal and cited supply shocks including energy.
- The July 28–29 minutes said nominal Treasury yields had risen 25 to 30 basis points over the intermeeting period, largely through higher real rates. The minutes also recorded stable longer-term inflation compensation and differing participant views about the appropriate year-end policy rate.
- The next scheduled FOMC meeting is September 15–16 and includes a Summary of Economic Projections.

## Energy supply

- The Energy Information Administration's August 26 report for the week ended August 21 showed U.S. commercial crude inventories rising 0.1 million barrels to 428.9 million, 1% above the five-year seasonal average.
- Motor-gasoline inventories fell 2.5 million barrels and were 6% below the five-year average; distillate inventories fell 2.2 million barrels and were about 14% below the five-year average.
- Four-week total petroleum products supplied averaged 20.5 million barrels per day, down 3.0% from the comparable year-earlier period. The EIA labels the weekly report as estimates and presents it alongside historical ranges.

## Corporate release available after the August 26 close

- NVIDIA reported fiscal second-quarter revenue of $96.2 billion, up 18% sequentially and 106% year over year. Data Center revenue was $89.0 billion, up 117% year over year. GAAP gross margin was 75.0% and GAAP diluted earnings per share were $2.46.
- NVIDIA forecast fiscal third-quarter revenue of $108.0 billion, plus or minus 2%, and a GAAP gross margin of 74.0%, plus or minus 50 basis points. The company said its outlook assumes no Data Center compute revenue from China. The company identifies the outlook as forward-looking and subject to business, supply, competition, regulatory, and macroeconomic risks.

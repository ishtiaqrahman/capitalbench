## Macro, Labor, Inflation, And Policy

- BLS reported June nonfarm payroll employment increased by 57,000. The unemployment rate was 4.2%, little changed.
- BLS reported the labor force participation rate decreased to 61.5%, and the employment-population ratio was 59.0%.
- BLS reported average hourly earnings rose 0.3% month over month in June to $37.64 and were up 3.5% over 12 months. The average workweek was 34.3 hours.
- BLS revised April payroll growth down from 179,000 to 148,000 and May down from 172,000 to 129,000, a combined downward revision of 74,000.
- BEA reported May current-dollar personal income up 0.7%, current-dollar disposable personal income up 0.7%, current-dollar PCE up 0.7%, and real PCE up 0.3%.
- BEA reported the May PCE price index up 0.4% month over month and core PCE up 0.3% month over month. Year over year, headline PCE was up 4.1% and core PCE was up 3.4%.
- On June 17, the Federal Reserve held the federal funds target range at 3.50% to 3.75% by a 12-0 vote.
- The June FOMC statement said economic activity was expanding solidly, while uncertainty was elevated by the Middle East conflict. It also said inflation remained elevated relative to the 2% goal, partly reflecting supply shocks including energy.

## Business Activity And Supply Chains

- ISM reported the June Manufacturing PMI at 53.3, down 0.7 point from May. Manufacturing new orders were 56.0, production 52.2, employment 49.7, supplier deliveries 57.4, and prices 73.0.
- ISM manufacturing comments included references to war involving Iran, tariff and pricing volatility, AI-related capacity consumption, and shortages in semiconductors and hot rolled steel.
- ISM reported the June Services PMI at 54.0, down 0.5 point from May. Services business activity was 55.4, new orders 55.1, employment 51.2, supplier deliveries 54.4, and prices 67.7.
- ISM services reported employment expanded for the first time in four months, while the prices index was still elevated at 67.7. Reported shortage categories included computers, electronic components, memory components, software licensing, steel, switchgear, transformers, and wire/cable.

## U.S. inflation and income

- The Bureau of Labor Statistics reported that headline CPI rose 0.1% in July and 3.4% over 12 months. CPI excluding food and energy rose 0.2% in July and 2.5% over 12 months.
- BLS reported that the Producer Price Index for final demand was unchanged in July and rose 4.7% over 12 months. July PPI values are preliminary.
- BLS reported that U.S. import prices fell 0.4% in July and export prices fell 1.3%. These price indexes exclude duties and can be revised.
- The Bureau of Economic Analysis reported that the PCE price index fell 0.1% in June and was 3.7% above June 2025. Personal income and disposable personal income each rose 0.2%, personal consumption expenditures rose 0.3%, and the personal saving rate was 2.7%. These estimates are subject to revision.

## U.S. labor, growth, and business activity

- BLS reported that total nonfarm payroll employment declined by 23,000 in July. The unemployment rate was 4.1% and the labor-force participation rate was 61.4%. May and June payroll changes were revised down by a combined 103,000. Payroll estimates remain subject to monthly and annual benchmark revision.
- BEA's advance estimate placed second-quarter real GDP growth at a 1.5% seasonally adjusted annual rate, after 2.1% in the first quarter. Real final sales to private domestic purchasers rose at a 3.9% annual rate. The second estimate is scheduled for August 26.
- ISM's July Manufacturing PMI was 55.6, with production at 58.5, new orders at 56.7, employment at 52.8, and prices at 71.1.
- ISM's July Services PMI was 54.1, with business activity at 59.1, new orders at 57.2, employment at 47.4, and prices at 70.3.
- The Federal Reserve reported that industrial production and manufacturing production each rose 0.2% in July. Total industrial production was 1.1% above its July 2025 level. Capacity utilization was 76.3%, 3.1 percentage points below its 1972–2025 average. July values are preliminary.

## Consumer and housing data

- Census reported July retail and food-services sales of $763.6 billion, down 0.6% from June and up 5.0% from July 2025. Sales excluding motor vehicles fell 0.3%; sales excluding motor vehicles and gasoline fell 0.2%. The headline monthly change has a ±0.4 percentage-point 90% confidence interval.
- Census reported second-quarter retail e-commerce sales of $340.2 billion, up 3.8% from the first quarter and 12.2% from a year earlier. E-commerce represented 17.1% of total retail sales. The quarter-over-quarter e-commerce estimate has a ±0.4 percentage-point 90% confidence interval; the year-over-year estimate has a ±0.9 percentage-point interval.
- Census and HUD reported July housing starts at a 1.239 million seasonally adjusted annual rate, down 12.4% from revised June and 13.5% from July 2025. The monthly change has a ±9.5 percentage-point confidence interval. Building permits were 1.443 million, up 5.0% from revised June and 3.1% from a year earlier. Housing completions were 1.212 million.
- The National Association of Realtors reported July existing-home sales at a 4.06 million annual rate, down 1.7% from June and up 0.7% from a year earlier. The median price was $434,100, up 2.0% over 12 months, and inventory was 1.54 million units.
- NAR reported that July pending home sales fell 2.3% from June and 2.2% from July 2025. Pending contracts do not always become completed sales.
- Home Depot reported fiscal-second-quarter sales of $47.86 billion and U.S. comparable-sales growth of 1.3%. It maintained fiscal-2026 total-sales growth guidance of 2.5% to 4.5%; that guidance is a company forecast.

## Company results released after the close

- Alphabet reported second-quarter revenue of $119.796 billion, up 24% from a year earlier. Google Services revenue rose 15%, Google Search and other advertising rose 17%, YouTube advertising rose 13%, and Google Cloud revenue rose 82% to $24.768 billion.
- Alphabet operating income rose 30% to $40.770 billion and operating margin was 34%, compared with 32%. Google Cloud operating income was $8.814 billion and Other Bets had a $1.799 billion operating loss.
- Alphabet reported $97.983 billion of other income, primarily unrealized equity-security gains. Net income was $112.107 billion. The results are unaudited, and unrealized gains may vary between periods.
- Tesla reported second-quarter revenue of $28.236 billion, up 26% from a year earlier. Automotive revenue was $20.516 billion, energy generation and storage revenue was $3.139 billion, and services and other revenue was $4.581 billion.
- Tesla's gross margin was 16.8%, compared with 17.2% a year earlier. Operating income was $398 million, operating margin was 1.4%, and GAAP net income attributable to common stockholders was $1.114 billion.
- Tesla reported $4.697 billion of operating cash flow, $5.789 billion of capital spending, negative $1.092 billion of free cash flow, and $43.524 billion of cash, cash equivalents, and short-term investments. The results are unaudited.

## U.S. economy and energy

- BLS reported June CPI down 0.4% for the month and up 3.5% over 12 months. CPI excluding food and energy was unchanged for the month and up 2.6% over 12 months.
- BLS reported June final-demand PPI down 0.3% for the month and up 5.5% over 12 months. Final demand excluding food, energy, and trade services rose 0.1% for the month and 5.1% over 12 months.
- June nonfarm payroll employment increased by 57,000, the unemployment rate was 4.2%, and average hourly earnings rose 3.5% over 12 months. April and May payroll changes were revised down by a combined 74,000.
- Census reported advance June retail and food-services sales up 0.2% from May and 6.7% from June 2025. The monthly estimate carried a plus-or-minus 0.4 percentage-point sampling interval and was not adjusted for price changes.
- Federal Reserve data showed June industrial production up 0.1% and capacity utilization at 76.1%. BEA's third estimate showed first-quarter real GDP up at a 2.1% annual rate.
- Census reported June housing starts at a 1.427 million annual rate, up 19.0% from revised May; the monthly estimate carried a plus-or-minus 15.9 percentage-point interval.
- For the week ended July 17, EIA reported commercial crude inventories excluding the Strategic Petroleum Reserve up 2.0 million barrels, gasoline up 0.8 million, distillates up 1.4 million, and total commercial petroleum inventories up 11.6 million.
- EIA reported refinery utilization at 96.1%. Four-week total product supplied averaged 20.4 million barrels per day, down 1% from a year earlier. Weekly estimates are preliminary and product supplied is a demand proxy.

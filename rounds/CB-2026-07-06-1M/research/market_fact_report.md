## Macro And Policy Facts

- BLS reported June 2026 nonfarm payroll employment changed little at +57,000 and the unemployment rate was 4.2%. Labor force participation decreased to 61.5%, and the employment-population ratio was 59.0%.
- BLS reported June average hourly earnings for all private nonfarm employees rose 0.3% month over month and 3.5% year over year. The average workweek was unchanged at 34.3 hours.
- BLS revised April payroll growth down from +179,000 to +148,000 and May down from +172,000 to +129,000, a combined downward revision of 74,000.
- BLS industry detail: professional and business services +36,000; social assistance +25,000; health care +22,000; leisure and hospitality -61,000. Other major industries changed little.
- BEA reported May personal income +0.7%, disposable personal income +0.7%, PCE +0.7%, real PCE +0.3%, and a personal saving rate of 3.0%.
- BEA reported May PCE prices +0.4% month over month and +4.1% year over year; core PCE prices +0.3% month over month and +3.4% year over year.
- The Federal Reserve's June 17 FOMC statement said the Committee voted 12-0 to maintain the target range for the federal funds rate at 3.50%-3.75%. The statement described economic activity as expanding at a solid pace and inflation as elevated relative to the 2% goal.

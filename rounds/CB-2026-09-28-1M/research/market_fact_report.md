## U.S. inflation, labor, and activity data

The August CPI release, published September 11, reported a 0.4% seasonally adjusted monthly increase in all-items CPI and a 3.4% increase over the 12 months through August. CPI excluding food and energy rose 0.3% in August and 2.4% over the year. BLS reported that gasoline rose 3.9% in August and accounted for over one-third of the monthly all-items increase [S5].

The August PPI release, published September 10, reported that final-demand prices rose 0.4% seasonally adjusted in August and 5.4% over the 12 months through August on an unadjusted basis. Final demand less food, energy, and trade services rose 0.3% in August and 4.7% over the year [S6]. BLS noted that its April–July figures were revised for late reports and respondent corrections.

The August Employment Situation, published September 4, reported a 162,000 increase in nonfarm payroll employment and an unemployment rate of 4.1%. BLS revised June payroll growth from 20,000 to 31,000 and July from a 23,000 decline to a 21,000 increase; the two months together were revised upward by 55,000 [S7]. BLS states that its monthly employment estimates are revised as additional employer and government reports arrive and seasonal factors are recalculated.

BEA's August 26 second estimate put real GDP growth in 2026 Q2 at a 1.5% annual rate, compared with 2.1% in Q1. Its release said increases in consumer spending, exports, and investment were partly offset by a decrease in government spending; the second estimate left the advance estimate of GDP growth unchanged [S8]. The latest monthly Personal Income and Outlays report available by the cutoff covered July: personal income rose 0.4% and personal consumption expenditures rose 0.2% [S9]. BEA scheduled its annual national-accounts update and the August Personal Income and Outlays release for September 30; the update can revise historical national-account estimates [S9, S10].

ISM's August survey results, released in early September, recorded a Manufacturing PMI of 54.6, down from 55.6 in July, and a Services PMI of 55.4, up from 54.1 in July. The Services Employment Index was 47.8 and its Prices Index was 72.6 [S11, S12]. ISM describes these as diffusion indexes from its business survey panels; they are survey readings rather than comprehensive counts of economic output.
